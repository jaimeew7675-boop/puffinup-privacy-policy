# Puffin Up — Support

Welcome to the official support page for Puffin Up!

Puffin Up is a retro-inspired arcade game where you guide a puffin through coastal obstacles, collect fish, and navigate changing weather.

### Need Help?

If you experience a bug, graphical issue, installation problem, or unexpected behavior, we're here to help.

**Email:** support@henryhop.com

When contacting us, please include:

- A brief description of the problem
- Your iPhone or iPad model
- Your iOS version
- The Puffin Up app version
- A screenshot, if helpful

Please do not send passwords or other sensitive information.

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Does Puffin Up require an internet connection?**

No. The game's core gameplay works offline.

**How do I control the puffin?**

Tap the screen to flap and navigate around obstacles.

**Does Puffin Up save my high score?**

Yes. Your best score is saved locally on your device.

**How do I turn off the sound?**

Tap the sound icon inside the game.

**Will uninstalling the game delete my progress?**

Deleting the application may also delete locally saved game progress.
