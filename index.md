# Puffin Up — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: November 22, 2026**

Puffin Up is a retro-style arcade game published by HenryHop. We respect your privacy and aim to keep the game simple, safe, and enjoyable.

### Information We Collect

Puffin Up does not require an account or ask players to provide personal information during gameplay.

The game does not intentionally collect names, addresses, contact information, or precise location data.

### Game Progress and Local Storage

Puffin Up stores certain information locally on your device, including:

- Personal best scores
- Fish collection progress
- Sound preferences

This information is used to maintain your gameplay experience and is not transmitted to our servers.

### Advertising and Analytics

Puffin Up does not currently include third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking technologies.

### Children's Privacy

Puffin Up is designed for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children through gameplay.

### Customer Support

If you contact us by email, we receive the email address and information you choose to provide. We use this information to respond to your request and handle support-related matters.

### Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy when the game or its features change. Any updates will be posted on this page.
